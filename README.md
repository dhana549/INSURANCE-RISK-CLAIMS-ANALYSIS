# Insurance Risk and Claims Analysis – Power BI Project  

## 1 Project Overview  
This project analyzes insurance policies and claims data to help an insurance company make data-driven decisions. The goal was to design a centralized interactive dashboard in Power BI that provides insights into customer profiles, claim patterns, and risk exposure.  

The dashboard enables stakeholders to  
1 Track key KPIs at a glance  
2 Identify risk-prone customer segments  
3 Understand claims behavior across demographics and car details  
4 Support business strategy, policy design, and risk management  

---

## 2 Business Requirements  

### 2.1 Key KPIs  
1 Total Policies – size of the active customer base  
2 Total Claim Amount – financial impact of claims  
3 Claim Frequency – frequency of filed claims  
4 Average Claim Amount – claim severity and risk exposure  
5 Gender-wise Total Policies – customer distribution by gender  

---

## 3 Dashboard Visualizations  

The dashboard uses two dynamic measures (Total Policies and Total Claim Amount) across multiple charts  

1 By Car Use (Donut Chart) – policy and claims distribution by personal vs commercial use  
2 By Car Make (Bar Chart) – car brands with higher claims and policies  
3 By Coverage Zone (Donut Chart) – regional risk analysis by geographic zone  
4 By Age Group (Histogram) – claim behavior across policyholder age brackets  
5 By Car Year (Area Chart) – impact of car’s manufacturing year on policies and claims  
6 By Kids Driving (Ribbon Chart) – effect of young drivers on claims and policies  
7 By Education (Pie Chart) – relationship between education level and insurance adoption  
8 By Education and Marital Status (Matrix Heat Grid) – combined effect on claims and policies  

---

3 Certain car makes consistently show higher claim severity  
4 Education and marital status combinations highlight unique customer risk profiles
